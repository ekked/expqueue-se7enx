# expqueue 0.1.0 (2026-10-11)

The first release: a generic, database-backed job queue for Exponential 6.

## Added

- Added: Seven tables (job, group, event, resource, class, mutex, worker) for MySQL/MariaDB, PostgreSQL, SQLite,
  Oracle and MongoDB from one definition, with `ext:expqueue:install-schema` (install, upgrade, check, print,
  write-files), `share/db_schema.dba` and `sql/<engine>/`.
- Added: The producer API `expQueue::enqueue()` with class, priority, group, idempotency key, resource, not-before,
  max attempts, timeout, dependency, user and site; groups; cancel; requeue; pause and resume per queue, class,
  resource and group; purge.
- Added: The handler contract (`expQueueHandler`, `expQueueHandlerBase`, `expQueueResult`, retryable and permanent
  exceptions) and the context: heartbeat and lease extension, cooperative cancellation, progress, the result
  checkpoint, rate-limit hints, cost, log lines.
- Added: The scheduler: stride scheduling over class weights, a reserved system class, aging, a starvation guard
  across and within classes, MinShare, MaxRunning per class, quotas per user and site, concurrency limits per
  resource that adapt to 429 / retry-after / remaining-requests hints, rules at enqueue time.
- Added: Leases with a compare-and-set claim, a reaper, retries with exponential backoff and jitter that honour
  retry-after, a dead letter, defer and hold.
- Added: Workers: `ext:expqueue:work`, the cron part `expqueue` (maintenance, hourly purge, a worker slice when no
  other worker is alive) and `ext:expqueue:service install|start|stop|status|uninstall` for systemd. Commands started
  as root become the site user; jobs never run in a web request or a Velocity worker.
- Added: The CLI `status`, `list`, `show`, `enqueue`, `cancel`, `pause`, `resume`, `retry`, `purge`, and the test
  job types `expqueue.noop`, `expqueue.sleep`, `expqueue.fail`.
- Added: Setup > Job queue: dashboard, job list, job and group pages with controls; policies `expqueue/view` and
  `expqueue/manage`; translations eng-US and ger-DE; the audit branch `system.queue.*`.

## Measured before the release (sandbox, SQLite, 12-core host under load 20-45)

- Scale run: 990 jobs (900 translation_full of which half on a resource limited to 1, 50 editorial on a resource
  limited to 2, 10 interactive, 10 failing jobs, 20 idempotency keys queued twice), 2 workers at once: queued in
  6.5 s (6.5 ms per enqueue), worked in 83 s (1,004 runs including retries). The 10 interactive jobs ended by 2.0 s,
  the 50 editorial ones by 7.8 s; 9 translation jobs ended while editorial ones still waited (no starvation). The
  limited resources never ran more than 1 and 2 jobs at once. Every job ended exactly once; 20 duplicates were
  returned; retryable failures went to the dead letter after 3 attempts with growing delays, a 429 with retry-after
  3 s was never retried sooner, and the resource got the cooldown.
- Fairness: 300 editorial, 100 bulk and 60 translation_full jobs at once, 1 worker: the first 150 starts were 76 %
  editorial, 13.3 % translation_full and 10.7 % bulk (stride targets 77 / 12.8 / 10.3 %). A priority-0 bulk job
  started after 18 s although 100 priority-100 bulk jobs waited (starvation guard).
- Reaper: a worker killed with kill -9 in the middle of a 6 s job: the lease expired, a second worker ran the job once
  more (events: enqueue, claim, reap, claim, done), attempts 2.
- SQLite contention: 400 no-op jobs, 2 workers: 42 jobs per second, no lost claim and no busy error.
